During a match the robot is rarely where a drawn path starts. The **pathfinder** plans a path from wherever the
robot is to a goal, around the obstacles in `field.json`, in a few milliseconds.

<Scene name="pathfind" caption="The planner on the 2026 field: the obstacles, the same shapes grown by the bumpers, the shortest route round them, and the smoothed path. Real follower, simulated drivetrain." />

## What it does at boot

`PathLibrary.loadAll()` runs this once, in robot init.

1. **Grow the obstacles.** Each one is grown by the robot's **reach**, the distance from its center to a bumper
   corner, plus a **margin**. The robot can then be treated as a point, at any heading.

   ```text
   reach = sqrt(length^2 + width^2) / 2
   grow  = reach + margin

   0.85 m square bumpers:  reach = 0.601 m
   margin 0.05 m:          grow  = 0.651 m
   ```

2. **Round the corners** with short straight pieces, placed slightly outside the true arc.
3. **Move the walls in** by the same grow distance.
4. **Build the visibility graph.** Every free corner of a grown shape is a node. Two nodes are joined when the
   straight line between them does not pass through the inside of any grown shape.

## What it does at go

`Pathfinder.plan` takes the robot's pose, its field velocity and the goal pose.

1. **Snap.** A start or goal inside a grown obstacle, or too close to a wall, is moved to the nearest free point.
   The path still starts at the robot and ends at the real goal.
2. **Search.** If the start can see the goal, the route is one straight line. Otherwise **A\***, a shortest-path
   search, finds the route through the graph. The same inputs always give the same route.
3. **Make waypoints.** The route's corners become waypoints of the same [curve](/concepts/the-curve/) a drawn
   path uses. A moving robot's first waypoint points along its direction of travel.
4. **Sweep.** The real bumper rectangle is placed along the path, at the planned heading, and the gap to every
   obstacle and wall is measured.
5. **Refit.** Each stretch closer than the margin gets a new waypoint from the straight route. Then the spline is
   fitted and swept again. Stretches next to a snapped start or goal are not checked.
6. **Plan the speed** with the same [speed plan](/concepts/speed-plan/) as a drawn path, from the robot's
   current speed to rest.

:::caution
`PlanResult.clear()` is false when 8 refits were not enough and the bumpers still come inside the margin. The
plan is returned anyway. Check `clear()` if you call the pathfinder yourself.
:::

## The numbers

| Number | Default | Where it lives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Margin | 0.05 m | `PlannerConfig.marginMeters` |
| Corner arc step | 30 degrees | `PlannerConfig.arcStepRadians` |
| Sweep spacing | 0.02 m | `PlannerConfig.checkStepMeters` |
| Refits allowed | 8 | `PlannerConfig.maxRefits` |
| Speed cap | 4 m/s | `PlannerConfig.maxMetersPerSecond` |
| Acceleration cap | 6 m/s² | `PlannerConfig.maxMetersPerSecondSquared` |
| Moving-start threshold | 0.1 m/s | fixed in `Pathfinder` |
| Hand-over approach | 0.5 m | `Pathfinder.APPROACH_METERS` |
| Narrowest gap with a route | 1.30 m | twice the grow distance, default robot at the 0.05 m margin |
| Grown shape oversize | at most 2.3 cm | default robot, from the 30 degree arc step |

## Timings

Measured on a desktop PC with other work running, not on a roboRIO or a Systemcore.

| What | Time |
| --- | --- |
| One plan, warmed up, 9.2 m route through six obstacles | 1.9 to 2.3 ms median, 4.8 to 8.1 ms at the 95th percentile |
| The same plan by part | search 0.08 ms, smoothing and sweep 1.7 ms, speed plan 0.15 ms |
| Plans on the three other bench routes | 0.47 to 1.0 ms median |
| First plan in a fresh JVM | 17 to 35 ms |
| Boot, growing and graph | 32 to 135 ms |

`PathLibrary` makes 40 throwaway plans at boot on each of the two bumper pathfinders, so the slow first plan does
not happen in a match. The six-obstacle test field's graph has 83 nodes.

In simulation, 300 random plans with random headings all kept the margin. 34 needed refits and the closest was
5.1 cm.

:::note
The library has not run on a real robot, and the robot controller has not been timed.
:::

## What it does not do

- **Moving obstacles.** Another robot in the way is not seen. A goal that moves is supported. See
  [Moving goals](/concepts/moving-goals/).
- **Concave shapes.** Each obstacle is replaced by its **convex hull**, the shape a rubber band makes around it.
- **Tight squeezes by turning.** Only a robot with a named [footprint](/concepts/footprints/) gets a second try
  at a gap it could pass nose first.
- **Tuning the curve.** Smoothing only adds waypoints.
