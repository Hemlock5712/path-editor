Picking up a game piece at speed means lining the intake up to within a few centimeters. The **piece assist**
keeps the driver's speed and turns their direction a little, so the intake arrives on the piece.

:::caution
This is a driver-feel feature. Its numbers come from the simulator, and none has been reviewed with a driver on a
robot. They are defaults, not recommendations.
:::

## What it does

[`PieceAssist`](/reference/follow/pieceassist/) chooses a piece and works out the nudge. Everything is measured
from the intake, not the robot's center.

1. A piece is a candidate when the stick speed is at least 0.3 m/s, the piece is within 3 m of the intake, and it
   is within 30° of the direction the stick is pushing.
2. The candidate most in line with the stick wins.
3. While last loop's piece is still a fair candidate, another takes over only if it is at least 10° more in line.
   A piece within 25 cm of last loop's choice is that piece again.
4. It measures how far **ahead** the piece is along the stick's direction, and how far to the **side**.
5. It works out a sideways slide, at right angles to the stick's direction, with no spin part.

   ```text
   slide = EAGERNESS · (side ÷ max(ahead, CLOSE_METERS)) · speed
   slide is held between −ASSIST_RATIO · speed and +ASSIST_RATIO · speed
   ```

6. `RailFollower.step(driver, nudge, dt)` adds the slide and scales the result back to the driver's own speed.
   Only the direction changes, by `atan(slide ÷ speed)`.
7. The result goes through the [output limiter](/concepts/output-limiter/), the same one teleop uses.

| Case | Turn |
| --- | --- |
| A piece 25 cm to the side and 2 m ahead | `atan(1.5 × 0.125)`, about 11° |
| At the cap | `atan(0.6)`, about 31° |

## Remembering pieces between photos

[`PieceFilter`](/reference/follow/piecefilter/) keeps one steady estimate per piece.

1. Robot code turns each detection into a spot on the floor in the robot's frame and hands over the whole photo.
2. The filter places it with the odometry pose at capture and keeps estimates in the wheel-odometry frame, like
   the [tag filter](/concepts/tag-filter/).
3. It gives each sighting a noise to the side and in range, for a two-dimensional Kalman filter. `e` is the
   angle error, `range` the floor distance from the lens, and `drop` the height of the lens above the piece's
   center.

   ```text
   error to the side  = e · range
   error in range     = e · (range² + drop²) ÷ drop
   ```

4. A detection matches a piece when it is within 15 cm to the side, or three times the combined noise if that is
   larger, and within 0.5 m along the line of sight.
5. Each piece is matched to at most one detection per photo. A detection that matches nothing starts a new piece.
6. A piece counts once it has been seen twice. A piece not seen for 0.5 s is forgotten.

| Example | Side error | Range error |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `e` of 0.3°, lens 0.33 m above the piece, piece 2 m away | about 1 cm | about 6.5 cm |

In simulation, the filter and the stay-with-a-piece rule together took the worst miss from between 20 and 45 cm
down to under 5 cm.

## The numbers

`PieceAssist`:

| Constant | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `MAX_ANGLE_RADIANS` | 30° | Pieces farther than this off the stick's direction are ignored. |
| `MAX_DISTANCE_METERS` | 3.0 m | Pieces farther than this from the intake are ignored. |
| `MIN_DRIVER_METERS_PER_SECOND` | 0.3 m/s | Below this stick speed there is no help. |
| `ASSIST_RATIO` | 0.6 | The slide is at most this share of the driver's speed. |
| `EAGERNESS` | 1.5 | How much more than "just in time" the slide is. At 1.5, two thirds of the gap is gone by halfway. |
| `CLOSE_METERS` | 0.20 m | Closer than this the slide stops growing. |
| `SAME_PIECE_METERS` | 0.25 m | A piece within this of last loop's choice is that piece again. |
| `SWITCH_RADIANS` | 10° | Another piece must be this much more in line to take over. |

`PieceFilter`:

| Constant | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `MEMORY_SECONDS` | 0.5 s | A piece not seen for this long is forgotten. |
| `SIGHTINGS_TO_COUNT` | 2 | A piece counts once seen this many times. |
| `SAME_PIECE_METERS` | 0.15 m | The sideways allowance for matching a detection to a piece. |
| `SAME_RANGE_METERS` | 0.5 m | The allowance along the line of sight. |
| `DRIFT_METERS` | 0.004 m | Uncertainty added to each piece before each photo. Private. |
| angle noise | 0.3° | The default camera angle noise, 1 sigma. Set in the constructor. |

`EAGERNESS` is the one number a team can pass in. `PieceAssist.nudge` and `RailDrive.towardPiece` both have a
form that takes it.

## What it does not do

- **It does not take over.** The nudge is a multiple of the driver's speed. With no stick there is no motion.
- **It does not pick a piece for the driver.** A piece behind the intake is never a candidate.
- **It does not turn the robot.** The intake has to be facing roughly the right way already.
- **It does not detect pieces, tell kinds apart, or avoid obstacles or other robots.**
